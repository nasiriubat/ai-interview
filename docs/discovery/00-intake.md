# Intake: raw idea as received

> Captured verbatim. The discovery process starts from this and never edits it.

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Submitted by:** Nasir (founder / product owner)

An AI powered discovery platform with two core workflows.

1. It interviews employees to understand daily work, identify pain points, and discover
   high value AI and automation opportunities across a company.
2. It also turns rough product ideas or discovered opportunities into clear, developer
   ready requirements through an adaptive AI interview, with structured specifications
   that can be handed directly to developers or coding agents like Codex and Claude Code.

**Meta-request:** build this system in the same manner the product itself would work,
using a "software farm" of role-based contributors. In other words, this repository
is the first customer of its own product: the idea above goes through the discovery
interview, becomes a structured spec, and that spec is what the team builds from.
