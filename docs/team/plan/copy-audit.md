# UX writing audit: `apps/web/src/i18n/en.ts` and `fi.ts`

Scope read in full: both locale files (1,194 leaf strings each; 72 are functions), `docs/spec/00`, `06`, `11`, `interviewer.v4.ts`, both seed templates, `packages/shared/src/settings.ts` (consent default and the 51-key catalog), `packages/core/src/privacy/retention.ts` (what deletion does), and the web components that render enum identifiers. No files were modified.

## 0. Three facts that override wording choices

1. **Deletion is narrower than the copy says.** `deleteSessionOps` removes messages, events, facts and attachments (and stored bytes), then sets the session to `status: deleted`, `state: {}`, `summary: ""`. The `Participant` row (`fields` JSON: name, role, department), the `Invitation` row (plain email on identified campaigns) and the session row itself stay. Copy must say "your answers, recordings and files", not "everything you said" or "your data".
2. **No deletion confirmation email exists.** `participant/service.ts#deleteData` calls `deleteSessionData` only; the only email code is `settings-email.ts` (test emails). `participant.session.deletedBody` promises an email. Until FR-13 is finished, the sentence must go.
3. **"Nothing is shared outside your organization" is not true** under NFR-02: content goes to the configured model, STT and TTS providers. Consent copy must name "the AI services your organization has chosen". Also not disclosed anywhere participant-facing: retention (default 24 months, audio 365 days), that admins may read identified transcripts (`privacy.rawTranscriptsVisibleTo` default), and that combined counts survive deletion (spec 11).

The default `campaign.consentText` in the catalog is English-only and 2 sentences; Finnish tenants currently see English consent unless they override. Proposed default (plain string, the field has no templating):

> **en:** "This interview is a conversation with an AI interviewer, run on behalf of your organization. Your answers, any files you attach and any voice recordings are kept so that your organization can understand how work is actually done. They are processed by the AI services your organization has chosen. You can pause, stop or delete your answers at any time from the interview menu; deleting removes your answers, recordings and files right away, while totals already combined with other people's answers remain and cannot be traced to you. Your data is deleted automatically after your organization's retention period."
> **fi:** "Tämä haastattelu on keskustelu tekoälyhaastattelijan kanssa, ja se tehdään organisaatiosi toimeksiannosta. Vastauksesi, liittämäsi tiedostot ja mahdolliset äänitallenteet säilytetään, jotta organisaatiosi ymmärtää, miten työ oikeasti tehdään. Niitä käsittelevät organisaatiosi valitsemat tekoälypalvelut. Voit keskeyttää, lopettaa tai poistaa vastauksesi milloin tahansa haastattelun valikosta. Poisto hävittää vastauksesi, tallenteesi ja tiedostosi heti; muiden vastauksiin jo yhdistetyt lukumäärät säilyvät, mutta niitä ei voi yhdistää sinuun. Tietosi poistetaan automaattisesti organisaatiosi säilytysajan jälkeen."

Recommendation: ship a localized default keyed by the campaign/template language (a small code change in the settings service), and revisit the "retention period" clause once a number can be interpolated.

## 3. Finnish quality review

**Register.** The whole file uses sinuttelu consistently; a scan for teitittely forms (te/teidän/teille/olette/voitte) found zero violations. Recommendation: keep sinuttelu everywhere, including admin. It is the norm for Finnish B2B SaaS and matches the interviewer's informal register.

**Verdict.** The Finnish is competent and mostly idiomatic; a Finnish reader would recognize it as translated in roughly one string in fifteen, concentrated in the admin area. Participant copy is close to native already. With the ~20 fixes below plus the terminology unification in section 2, it reads as native product copy.

Anglicisms and literal translations (fix): Kojelauta (Dashboard) → Yleiskatsaus; Edistetty (Promoted) → Määrittelyssä; Siirtotiivistelmä (Hand-off) → Lähtötiedot; Tapahtumien toisto (Event replay) → Tapahtumaloki; Ominaisuusliput → Ominaisuudet; Ylikirjoitettu/ylikirjoitus → Asetettu tässä / oma arvo; Generoitu/generoida (10 occurrences) → tuotettu/luotu; Haastattelija vastusti kerran → esitti vastaväitteen kerran; Vastaus latautuu → Haastattelija vastaa; Moottori → Haastattelumoottori (or drop); erotuksineen → "vanha ja uusi arvo"; Hallinnan navigaatio → Hallinnan valikko; Reititys → Mallivalinnat; selkokieli (a regulated register for people with reading difficulties) → yleiskieli; Haastattelun toteuttaa X → Alusta: X (changes meaning); Aloitetaan linkistäsi → Aloita kutsulinkistä.

Compound and case issues: "OpenAI-API-avain" → "OpenAI:n API-avain"; "spec.jsonin" → "spec.json-tiedoston"; "Lainaukset näytöksi" → "Lainaukset näyttönä"; "Koskettaa työntekijöitä" → "Työntekijöitä, joita koskee"; "Osioita sisällöllä" → "Täytettyjä osioita"; "antaaksesi suostumuksen ja aloittaaksesi" → split into clauses (two stacked -ksesi forms read as officialese); "Sijoitus 72 / 100" wrong semantics → "72 / 100 pistettä". Correct and worth keeping: "OpenAI:ta", "OpenAI:hin", "Mt" for MB, "0,017 dollaria", "verkkotunnus".

Internal inconsistencies in fi (en is consistent): Peruuta/Peru for Revoke; Keskeytä used for both Pause and Suspend; Avattu vs Esitetty for raised; Siirretty vs Lykätty for deferred; Laajuus vs Taso for scope; Istunto vs Haastattelu for session (campaigns say Istunnot, crumbs say Haastattelut); Havainnot used for both facts and findings; "Hae pohjia" vs "Mallipohjat".

## 5. Voice and tone guide (one page)

**Who is speaking.** The product speaks as "we" and is calm, concrete and brief. The interviewer is a third party the product introduces ("The interviewer is thinking"); the product never says "we" about something the interviewer does. The interviewer itself speaks in the first person inside the chat (prompt v4) and is warm, brief and specific; UI text next to it must not be louder or chattier than it is.

**Principles**

1. Say what happened, what we kept, what to do next, in that order. Every error has all three or is a single word.
2. Promise only what the code does. If a sentence cannot be verified against the implementation (deletion scope, emails, who sees what), cut it.
3. No internal nouns in front of participants: session, campaign, purpose, route, engine, widget, cluster, fact, delta, worker, token, shipped. Admins may see "template", "campaign", "provider", "budget", "task"; never "purpose", "route", "scope", "tenant" (except operators).
4. Plain verbs over nouns: "Deleting removes…" not "Deletion is immediate." "Who can answer" not "Verification."
5. Specific over generic: "Your answers are saved" beats "Nothing was lost." Numbers and names where we have them.
6. Calm, not cheerful. No exclamation marks, no "Great!", no "please" padding, no marketing adjectives (seamless, powerful, smart). Gratitude once, at the end.
7. Empty states teach the next step in one sentence; destructive confirmations name the consequence, not the mechanism.
8. One term per concept (section 4). Labels are nouns; buttons are verbs; status chips are adjectives or participles.
9. Finnish: sinuttelu, yleiskieli, inflect the product's nouns rather than quoting them, avoid stacked -ksesi/-kseen forms, write numbers Finnish style (0,017; 5 Mt).
10. Accessibility text (aria, live regions) describes state, not UI chrome: "The interviewer is answering", not "Answer is streaming".

**Ten before / after**

1. "Your data was deleted. … a confirmation email is on its way." → "Your answers were deleted. Your answers, recordings and files from this interview are gone."
2. "Nothing is shared outside your organization." → "Your organization's admins can read them, and the AI services your organization uses process them."
3. "Nothing was lost. Try again in a moment." → "Your answers are saved. Try again in a moment."
4. "The campaign has ended and no longer accepts answers." → "This interview round has ended and no longer takes answers."
5. "Answer is streaming" → "The interviewer is answering"
6. "This removes all your answers, extracted facts and any attachments right away." → "This removes your answers, recordings, files and everything noted from them right away."
7. "Every knob from the specification, grouped by category." → "Everything you can adjust, grouped by category."
8. "Routing per purpose" → "Model assignment per task"
9. "No sessions yet." → "No interviews yet. They appear here as soon as someone opens the link."
10. fi: "Haastattelija vastusti kerran" → "Haastattelija esitti vastaväitteen kerran"; fi: "Kojelauta" → "Yleiskatsaus"

## 6. Counts and systemic patterns

Existing strings proposed to change: **en 142** (participant/auth 58, admin 84), **fi 171** (participant/auth 55, admin 116; the extra fi rows are consistency fixes where en is already uniform). New strings proposed: **~75 enum labels per locale** (section 2b) plus **102 per locale** if the settings catalog is localized. One shape change: `participant.session.skipMessage` becomes a function. One data change: `campaign.consentText` default, ideally per language.

Top 5 systemic patterns

1. **Code nouns leaking into copy** (session, campaign, purpose, route, engine, shipped, worker, cluster, hand-off, extracted facts, scope, tenant): ~60 strings, the single largest class.
2. **Promises the implementation does not keep**: deletion scope, confirmation email, "nothing shared outside", "edit before it is stored", retention and transcript visibility undisclosed. Eight strings plus the consent default; legal priority.
3. **Rank/score and status vocabularies**: "rank" for a 0–100 score across 9 strings; three separate status maps (list, findings, results) that drift in Finnish.
4. **`humanize()` as a labelling strategy**: ~22 rendering sites and the entire Settings page show English identifiers in the Finnish UI.
5. **Empty states and errors that state rather than guide**: 14 empty states say "No X yet." without the next step; 6 participant errors omit what was kept or what to do.

Decisions for the owner: (a) "campaign/kampanja" vs "interview round/haastattelukierros" in admin UI (participants already get the latter above); (b) whether to localize the settings catalog now or keep it English with a note; (c) whether to ship the deletion email (restores the `deletedBody` sentence) or remove the Participant/Invitation identity rows on delete, which would allow "Your data was deleted" again.
