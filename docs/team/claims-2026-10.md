# Claims inventory, October 2026

What the product may say about itself, re-checked against the code at the tip of
`claude/ai-discovery-platform-bzxmqw` (after "docs: tracker after M3"). It replaces section 0 of
[plan/positioning.md](plan/positioning.md), which was written on 2026-10-13 against a tree that has
since gained erasure, PDF export, notifications, department comparison, participant export, RLS on
every table, rate-limit identity, alerting, prompt administration and template rollback.

**Rule for the landing page and for sales material: say what is `Built`, in the present tense; say
`Partial` only with the qualifier in the "Say it this way" column; never put `Planned` in the
present tense, and do not mention it at all on the landing page.** The landing page
(`apps/web/src/i18n/landing.en.ts`, `landing.fi.ts`) is tested against this rule: a test fails when
it names SSO, Slack, Teams, Jira, webhooks, billing, certification, collaboration, GitHub or "nothing
leaves" (`apps/web/test/landing.test.tsx`).

How a status was assigned. **Built**: the behaviour exists and a test, a route or a document in the
repository shows it. **Partial**: it exists, but one part of the sentence people would hear is not
shown, or it has only been exercised against a fake. **Planned**: not in the code, or only in the
spec.

Evidence paths are relative to the repository root. "Test" names the file; the suite runs in
`pnpm check` with a database unless stated otherwise.

## What this review found that the old inventory did not say

1. The "you can raise it" claim about k was wrong in one direction: the setting accepts 1 to 50 and
   a tenant can lower it to 1, which removes the protection. The landing page says the organization
   sets the number. Consider a floor of 2 or 3 at the platform level (a decision for the owner).
2. The report said "Quotes and breakdowns appear only where at least k participants are behind
   them" only under "Participation and method". The cover now says it first, but only in English:
   `renderReport` is English whatever the analysis language is. A Finnish cover needs the report's
