# Screenshot index

Desktop shots are 1440x900 at 2x (2880x1800). Mobile shots are 390x844 (the arrival at 2x,
the chat at 3x as shot). PDF pages are rendered at 2x. Everything was taken on 2026-10-14 from
the running app against a throwaway database with fictional data (company "Harbourline Clinics",
nine invented colleagues played by a language model). The sidebar shows the demo staff address
`live-admin@example.test`; crop it out if you do not want it in frame.

"Best moment" is where it earns its place in the voice-over (scene ids refer to
`../voiceover/script.txt`).
