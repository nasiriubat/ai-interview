Most teams decide what to automate from a hunch, a vendor demo, or the loudest person in the room. There is a better place to start: the people who do the work.

You set up a discovery campaign from a template, write a short welcome, and share one link, or a QR code, with your team.

Your colleagues open it on a laptop or a phone. No account. They see who is asking and why, what is kept, and how to delete it, before they agree to anything.

Then an AI interviewer asks about their actual week. One question at a time. It follows the same template for everyone, and it is instructed never to ask where AI should go. It asks what fills the day, and what gets in the way.

They can type, tap an answer option, or simply record an answer, where voice is set up.

At the end, they see a summary they can correct. They can download what they said, or delete it.

Now the part a chat window cannot give you. Every interview is turned into items on a fixed schema, then grouped, counted and scored across the whole campaign. In this demo: six simulated colleagues, nine groups.

Each opportunity is scored on seven factors, with the reasons. And sometimes the answer is not AI. Here it says why plain automation fits better. That is a result, too.

Departments are compared only when at least three people, the default, are behind a number. Below that, it is withheld. By design.

The report states its method, and what it does not do, and exports as a PDF you can hand to leadership.

When something is worth building, one click starts a requirements interview, carrying over what the interviews found. It asks one decision at a time, suggests a sensible default, and pushes back when something does not add up.

Before it finishes, it reads back what it understood, so the owner can correct it.

The result is a versioned specification with numbered requirements, and you can see exactly what changed between versions.

Download it as a PDF, as Markdown, or as a bundle for coding agents like Claude Code or Codex.

It is built to run on a server you operate, with the AI providers you choose, and every model call is recorded with its cost. Understand the problem, before deciding what to build.
